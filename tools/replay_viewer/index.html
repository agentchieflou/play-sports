About this play

What you are seeing

This play is synthetic. Its positions, speeds and events were made by hand from waypoints to test the viewer; nothing in it comes from the game. The notes below describe a real recording.

  • Where every player and the ball was, how fast and which way each moved and faced, and who had the ball: the game's telemetry sampler recorded these, frame by frame.
  • Between two recorded frames, positions are blended the way the game's own replay blends them.
  • Moments on the timeline are the events the game's telemetry bus announced. A hollow marker is read off the frames instead (the ball changing hands).
  • Contacts are opponents whose collision capsules (44 cm radius) touch, measured from their positions.
  • The viewer draws the bodies: a stand-in character whose run cycle follows each player's recorded speed and ground covered, sized by height and weight when the roster has them. Skin, hair and kit colours are presentation only.
